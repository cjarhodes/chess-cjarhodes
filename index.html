How to read this evidence

Sessions and attempts record activity. Coaching flags are estimates, not proof of learning. Counts use up to 300 retained reviews; imported games use the date they were reviewed.

After practice compares 30 reviewed moves before an unassisted completion with the first 30 after it. Rehearsing again keeps that checkpoint while its baseline is retained. A lower flag rate does not prove practice caused a change.
