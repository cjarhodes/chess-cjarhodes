Design system · D · Blend

Principles

  • One primary button per screen state. Everything else is secondary, quiet, or in a menu.
  • Say it on the board first. Arrows, tints and quality glyphs; the panel explains why.
  • Colour never stands alone. Move quality always carries its glyph or word.
  • Three typefaces, three jobs. Fraunces for titles, Newsreader for coach sentences, DM Sans for controls; DM Mono only for moves, FEN and numbers.
  • Tokens only. Components never use a raw colour, size, radius or font.

Type scale

Seven sizes replace the 47 in today's stylesheet. Coach text is always Newsreader at --text-prose.
